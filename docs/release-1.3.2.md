# willGo 1.3.2

New questions are inserted at the beginning of the question list and selected.
Older questions move down. This applies to finalized speech, microphone trigger
questions, typed/phone questions, region screenshots and phone screenshots.
Answers remain attached to their original entries; completing or retrying an
answer does not reorder questions. Ctrl+Shift+Up moves toward newer questions;
Ctrl+Shift+Down moves toward older questions, following visible list order.

The login gate, manual speech switch, opacity shortcuts and mouse passthrough
retain their existing behavior.
